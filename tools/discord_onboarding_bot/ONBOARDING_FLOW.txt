Discord onboarding and Hackathon role flow:
1. New members get a tagged copy of the pinned #hackathon-intros template.
2. Both the pinned template and each personal copy have ✅ and ❌ reactions.
3. ✅ adds the existing Hackathon role.
4. ❌ removes the Hackathon role.
5. Personal copies are deleted when the user posts in #hackathon-intros or after 24 hours.
